## Hello. My name is Amoge Ndukwe
I’m a user researcher and service designer who codes and ships things that people and teams actually use.

Right now I work on government digital services. I provide the evidence for what people actually need, what the government needs to build, and use AI to build and test faster. I've done this work across teams in Nigeria, Ghana, Cote d'Ivoire, and Barbados.  

## What I believe
Launching a service is not the end of the job. It matters whether people use it,
whether it works for them, and whether the team can keep improving it on their own.
I'm learning to measure that better, and I write on [[BrainStack](https://amogendukwe.substack.com/)] about what I'm learning along
the way.
